The honest version

  • The shortage is mostly for experienced people. Entry-level security jobs get a lot of applicants, and in ISC2's 2025 survey 24% of security teams had layoffs and 36% had budget cuts in the past year.
  • Most people get in through IT first. Help desk, system administration or development experience is the usual route, and the roadmaps show where those jobs fit.
  • Teams say they are short of skills more than headcount. AI and cloud security topped the list of skills they need.

How the roadmaps work

  1. Pick a roadmap. Start with cyber security if you're new. Pen testing builds on it.
  2. Click any box. You'll see what it is, how it's used on the job, what to learn, and free resources.
  3. Mark your progress. Done, learning or skip. It's saved in this browser only; there are no accounts.
  4. Stop at job checkpoints. They show which jobs you could apply for at that point, with real pay data.

Only practise on systems you own or have written permission to test. Unauthorised access is a crime in Canada, the US and most other countries.
