What this is, and isn't

  • Every question was written for this site. None are copied from a real exam, and they won't match what you'll see on exam day. They're here to check that you understand the ideas.
  • Hands-on exams such as eJPT, PNPT, CPTS and OSCP+ are passed in a lab, not by recall. Use these sets alongside the study plans and legal practice labs.
  • Questions cover concepts, judgement and defence. Hands-on skills are practised on platforms built for it, listed on the roadmap.
