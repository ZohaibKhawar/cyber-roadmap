Penetration testing

Pen testers are hired to find weaknesses before criminals do, then explain them clearly enough that they get fixed. This roadmap covers the skills, the practice, the reporting and the certifications that lead to the job.

$116,580 US median pay for the group pen testers are counted in 2025 · O*NET, from BLS "computer occupations, all other"
5–6% Projected growth for that group, 2025–35: faster than average O*NET

OSCP+ The hands-on certification many pen test job ads ask for Compare every cert and its price →

Permission first, always. Testing a system without the owner's permission can be a crime in Canada and the US, even if you don't change anything. Get that permission in writing before you start, so you can prove it. Practise on your own lab, on platforms built for practice, or on bug bounty programs inside their published scope.
