Certifications, compared

Certificates get your resume past filters; skills get you through the interview. Here's what each one proves, roughly what it costs, and when it's worth paying for.

A common order for pen testing

  1. Security+

    One of the certs entry-level security ads name most. Multiple choice.

  2. eJPT

    A first hands-on pen test exam. Cheap and beginner friendly.

  3. PNPT or CPTS

    Realistic multi-day exams with a full written report.

  4. OSCP+

    The hands-on cert many pen test job ads ask for.

  5. Specialise

    OSWE or BSCP for web, OSEP or CRTO for red teaming.

You don't need all of them. Many testers skip steps 2 or 3. For blue team jobs, swap steps 2–4 for CySA+, BTL1 or SC-200. Week-by-week study plans for six of these →

Before you pay for anything

  • Search job ads in your own city and count which certs they name. That beats any list, including this one.
  • Hands-on exams (you hack or investigate a real lab) impress technical interviewers more than multiple-choice ones. HR filters and government jobs often want the multiple-choice ones by name.
  • Employers often pay for certs once you're hired. Ask before buying expensive ones yourself.
  • Prices change and sales are common. Figures below are approximate list prices in US dollars before tax.
